Getting Started 🌱
Welcome to the Did Today admin tool. This walks through everything you'll actually use, in the order you'll use it. Nothing here requires any technical background — if you can use a phone, you can use this. Reach out to Dave any time you get stuck.
1Your first login
Dave will text you a personal link — that's your way in, and it's just for you. The very first time you open it, you'll be asked to pick a 4-digit PIN.
Pick anything easy for you to remember. After that first time, your device remembers you — you won't see this screen again unless you're on a new phone or computer.
Why a PIN at all? This tool has real client contact info and check-in history behind it. The PIN means that even if your link ever got seen by someone else, your data stays protected.
2Your roster
This is home base — every time you open your link, this is what you'll land on. Each client gets a card showing their week at a glance.

Needed help
Tap "+ Add my client" to bring someone new on board — it just takes their name and phone number, and it hands you their real link to send them.
3A client's plan
Tap any client's card to see exactly what they're tracking — what Dave and the team call their "dids."
Tap any assigned did to adjust their schedule or goal for it. Tap "+ Assign an item" to add something new from the library — or create something brand new on the spot if it's not in there yet.
4The Check-In Library
This is the full menu — everything any client could possibly be tracking, organized by category. Some of it's shared, some is specific to Studio Strong or Premier U, and the tabs at the top let you filter to just what's relevant to you.
Tap "+ Add new item" any time you want to start tracking something that isn't already here — just describe it, and it gets added for everyone to use going forward.
What your client actually sees
Worth knowing, since they'll ask you about it: their side is dead simple by design. They open their own link, tap what they did that day, and they're done — usually under a minute. They get a short motivational line back, and if they're having a rough day, one tap sends you a real email so you know to check in on them.
The whole philosophy is no judgment — this isn't about catching anyone slipping. It's just honest tracking, so the things that actually move their life forward don't quietly fall through the cracks.
A couple things worth knowing
I got a new phone — why is it asking me to set a PIN again?
That's expected. Your PIN is remembered per device, not stored anywhere else. Just set a new one the same way you did the first time.
I forgot my PIN.
Text Dave — he can look up what's on file for you, or help you reset it.
Can I mess something up?
Not really — nothing here deletes client history, and everything's editable. If something looks off, just ask.
